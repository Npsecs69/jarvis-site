Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

JARVIS is a personal desktop AI assistant used for private development and testing.

Information accessed

When a user authorizes TikTok access, JARVIS may access information permitted by the approved scopes, such as public video information and account statistics.

How information is used

Authorized information is used only to provide requested analytics and assistant features to the account owner.

Sharing

JARVIS does not sell authorized TikTok information or use it for advertising.

Access and revocation

The user may revoke the application's TikTok access through TikTok account settings or by discontinuing use of the application.

Contact

For questions about this development project, contact the application owner through the account associated with this site.
